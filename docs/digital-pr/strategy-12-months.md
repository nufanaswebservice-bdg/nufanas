## Channel Strategy

### Instagram (@nufanas)
- **Frequency:** 3x/minggu + daily stories
- **Content Mix:**
  - 40% Educational (tips, tutorial carousel)
  - 30% Showcase (portfolio, before-after)
  - 20% Behind the scenes (tim, proses kerja)
  - 10% Promotional (CTA, offering)

### LinkedIn (Nufanas Digital Agency)
- **Frequency:** 2-3x/minggu
- **Content Mix:**
  - 50% Thought leadership articles
  - 30% Case studies & insights
  - 20% Company updates & hiring

### YouTube (@nufanas)
- **Frequency:** 2x/bulan
- **Content:**
  - Tutorial web development
  - SEO tips untuk bisnis lokal
  - Client testimonial
  - Tech review & comparison

### TikTok (@nufanas)
- **Frequency:** 3-5x/minggu
- **Content:**
  - Quick tips (15-60 detik)
  - Before-after website redesign
  - Meme/trend adaptasi (tech version)
  - Day in the life

### Facebook
- **Frequency:** 3x/minggu
- **Content:**
  - Group engagement (jawab pertanyaan)
  - Share blog posts
  - Community polls
  - Event promotion
