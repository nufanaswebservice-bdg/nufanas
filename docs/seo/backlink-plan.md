## 3. Strategi Implementasi

### Bulan 1-2: Foundation
- Setup semua profile links (20 links)
- Submit ke 15 directory Indonesia
- Buat 10 blog di Web 2.0 platforms
- Submit 5 local citations

### Bulan 3-4: Growth
- Publish guest post ke 10 medium-authority blogs
- Submit ke 10 startup directories
- Kirim 5 press release
- Buat 15 forum contributions

### Bulan 5-6: Authority
- Publish guest post ke 10 high-authority blogs
- Submit ke remaining directories
- Complete all local citations
- Build relationship untuk recurring backlinks

## 4. Anchor Text Strategy

### Distribution Target:
- Branded (40%): "Nufanas", "Nufanas Digital Agency"
- Naked URL (20%): "nufanas.com", "https://nufanas.com"
- Generic (15%): "klik di sini", "baca selengkapnya", "website ini"
- Exact Match (10%): "jasa website bandung", "web developer bandung"
- Partial Match (10%): "jasa pembuatan website di bandung", "agency digital di bandung"
- LSI/Long-tail (5%): "layanan web development profesional area bandung"

## 5. Monitoring & KPI

### Tools:
- Google Search Console (GSC)
- Ahrefs / SEMrush
- Moz Link Explorer

### Monthly KPI:
- Jumlah backlink baru: min 15-20/bulan
- Domain Rating growth: +2-3 per bulan
- Referring domains: +10-15/bulan
- Organic traffic growth: +20-30%/bulan

---

## 6. DO NOT DO (Blackhat Tactics to Avoid)
- ❌ PBN (Private Blog Network)
- ❌ Link farms
- ❌ Paid links tanpa nofollow
- ❌ Comment spam
- ❌ Auto-generated content untuk link building
- ❌ Excessive reciprocal links
- ❌ Hidden links
- ❌ Doorway pages
